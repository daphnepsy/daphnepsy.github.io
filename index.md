Hi! My name is Daphne, and I was born and raised in Hong Kong before coming to the US for college. I'm happiest when I get to work on projects at the intersection of engineering, computer science, and robotics, and I'm especially excited about solving problems around community and urbanism. I studied Computer Science and Math at Harvey Mudd College, and now I'm working at Waymo, helping self-driving cars make better lane changes. I strive to better the world through my work. Since graduating, I've bounced around San Francisco, London, and finally New York City, where I now live with my [husband](https://dipaolo.xyz/).

In my free time, I enjoy [baking](https://www.instagram.com/daphgetsbaked/), climbing, reading, and hosting friends in my tiny NYC apartment.
