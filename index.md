Hi! My name is Daphne, I was born and raised in Hong Kong, before coming to the US for college. I'm my happiest when I get to work on projects at the intersection of engineering, computer science, and robotics. I studied Computer Science and Math at Harvey Mudd College, and since then I've been working at Waymo, helping self-driving cars make better lane changes. I strive to better the world through my work, and am especially excited about solving problems around community and urbanism. Since graduating, I've bounced around San Francisco, London, and finally New York City, where I now live with my [husband](https://dipaolo.xyz/).

In my free time, I like to do yoga, rock climb, and call friends and family.
